Welcome to Henderson

Henderson is Nevada’s second-largest city and one of the fastest-growing communities in the country. In the southeastern Las Vegas Valley, it blends suburban comfort with urban convenience — the Strip is typically about 25 minutes away — and is widely known for safety, parks, and strong schools.

Established in the 1940s around magnesium production for World War II, Henderson has grown into a full city of master-planned communities, mature neighborhoods, championship golf, and more than 180 miles of trails. It consistently ranks among the safer larger cities in the U.S. and attracts families and professionals seeking a high quality of life.

Lifestyle & community

Life here is family-focused: outdoor recreation, community events, and education. Annual events such as WinterFest and the Henderson Heritage Parade, plus farmers markets and recreation centers, reinforce a strong local identity. The climate is classic Mojave Desert — hot, dry summers and mild winters with more than 300 sunny days a year.

Housing & real estate

Median prices generally run above the broader Las Vegas metro average, with options from condominiums into multi-million-dollar estates. Quality construction and master planning are hallmarks.

  • Green Valley — One of Henderson’s first master-planned communities; mature landscaping, strong schools, mix of housing styles ($350K–$800K typical range for many homes).
  • MacDonald Highlands — Guard-gated luxury in the McCullough Mountains with Strip views, country club living, and Dragon Ridge Golf Course ($1M–$15M+).
  • Inspirada — Newer master-planned living focused on outdoor connectivity, parks, trails, and modern designs ($300K–$700K).

Demand stays steady because of safety, schools, and amenities. New construction continues in southern and eastern sections with an emphasis on energy efficiency and modern plans.

Schools

Henderson is served by Clark County School District and is known for some of Nevada’s higher-performing public schools, plus private and charter options. Families often prioritize specific elementary and high school zones when choosing a neighborhood — Coronado High School is among the frequently requested names.

Amenities, dining & shopping

Parks, trails, recreation centers, and golf are major draws. Lake Las Vegas and the Village at Lake Las Vegas add waterfront dining and boutiques. The local dining scene has matured beyond chains into chef-driven and neighborhood favorites — including spots known for Neapolitan pizza, brunch, French bistro fare, and acclaimed Thai cuisine.

At a glance

  • Population: 320,000+
  • Median household income: roughly $75K
  • Crime rates significantly lower than national averages for many comparable cities
  • Cost of living modestly higher than the Las Vegas average
  • Commute to the Strip: about 25 minutes

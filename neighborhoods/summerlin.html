Summerlin

Las Vegas’ premier master-planned community — villages, parks, trails, and proximity to Red Rock Canyon.

Summerlin remains one of the most consistently sought-after addresses in the Las Vegas Valley. Designed as a series of villages with distinct character, the community offers a full spectrum of housing — from entry-level townhomes to gated custom estates in The Ridges and Summit Club.

Lifestyle & Amenities

Residents enjoy extensive parks, trail systems, the Downtown Summerlin shopping and entertainment district, multiple golf courses, and easy access to outdoor recreation at Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area. The community was planned with long-term livability in mind.

Market Position

Summerlin typically commands a premium relative to many other Las Vegas submarkets because of its amenities, school options, and perceived quality of life. Inventory and pricing vary significantly by village — The Ridges and similar luxury pockets operate on a different plane than more moderately priced villages.

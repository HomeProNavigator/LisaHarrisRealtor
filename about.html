About Lisa

Focused representation for buyers and sellers who value clarity, local expertise, and a single point of contact.

Lisa Harris
Salesperson · NV S.0059389

I work as a solo agent by design. That means when you hire me, you work directly with me — from the first conversation through closing and beyond. There is no handoff to a junior team member or call center.

My practice is centered on Las Vegas and Henderson, with particular depth in Summerlin, Green Valley, Anthem, MacDonald Highlands, and the surrounding luxury and established communities. I stay close to the micro-markets so pricing advice, buyer strategy, and negotiation are grounded in current data rather than broad averages.

Whether you are relocating to Southern Nevada, upsizing into a view lot, or preparing a home for a discreet sale, the goal is the same: clear information, disciplined process, and representation that protects your interests.

Welcome to Henderson

Henderson is consistently ranked among the safest large cities in the United States and is a primary choice for families relocating to Southern Nevada. Located southeast of the Las Vegas Strip, it offers master-planned communities, extensive parks and trails, and a strong sense of local identity separate from the resort corridor.

Communities such as Green Valley, Anthem, MacDonald Highlands, Inspirada, and Cadence give buyers options from established neighborhoods to newer construction with modern floor plans and amenities.

Lifestyle & community

Henderson emphasizes quality of life: recreation centers, sports complexes, open space, and community events. Lake Las Vegas adds water recreation and resort dining nearby. Residents often cite lower crime rates, better school options, and a more suburban rhythm than central Las Vegas.

The city has invested heavily in trails, parks, and multimodal transportation. Many neighborhoods are designed so daily errands and school drop-off stay close to home.

Housing & real estate

Pricing spans entry-level townhomes through luxury estates in MacDonald Highlands and custom homes near Lake Las Vegas. New construction remains active in southern and eastern growth areas.

  • Green Valley — Established master plan with mature landscaping, parks, and a mix of single-family and attached product.
  • Anthem — Hillside living with mountain views, recreation centers, and a range of housing types.
  • Inspirada — Newer master-planned community with modern designs, parks, and a walkable town center focus.
  • MacDonald Highlands — Guard-gated luxury with elevated views toward the Strip and mountains.

Schools

Henderson is served by Clark County School District and is widely regarded as having some of the stronger public school options in the valley, plus private and charter schools. Families commonly research specific elementary, middle, and high school ratings by neighborhood and address — zoning can change, so verify for any property under consideration.

Amenities, dining & shopping

The District at Green Valley Ranch, Lake Las Vegas, and multiple neighborhood shopping centers provide dining and retail. Recreation includes multi-use trails, sports complexes, and proximity to Lake Mead National Recreation Area. Henderson Pavilion hosts concerts and community events.

At a glance

  • Population: 320,000+
  • Strong reputation for safety and family amenities
  • Mix of established and new-construction communities
  • Commute to the Strip: roughly 15–25 minutes depending on location

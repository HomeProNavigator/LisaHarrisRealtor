MacDonald Highlands

One of Henderson’s premier luxury addresses — gated, golf-oriented, and defined by dramatic topography and architecture.

MacDonald Highlands is a private, gated community known for its golf course, elevated lots, and high-end custom and semi-custom homes. It attracts buyers seeking privacy, views, and a more exclusive setting within Henderson.

Luxury Positioning

Properties here typically sit at the upper end of the Henderson market. View corridors, lot size, architectural quality, and proximity to the club are key value drivers. The community has a distinct identity separate from more mainstream neighborhoods.

Representation Notes

Transactions in MacDonald Highlands often benefit from a measured, discreet approach. Pricing strategy and buyer qualification are especially important in a market segment where both buyers and sellers tend to be sophisticated.

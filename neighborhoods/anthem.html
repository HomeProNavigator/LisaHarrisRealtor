Anthem Real Estate

Elevated Henderson living with mountain views, country club amenities, and a range of luxury and executive homes.

Anthem sits in the southern foothills of Henderson and is known for its topography, views, and the presence of Anthem Country Club. The community offers a clear step up in lifestyle and often in price relative to many flatter parts of the valley.

Views, Golf & Community

Many homes take advantage of elevated lots with mountain or valley vistas. The country club provides golf, dining, and social amenities that are central to the area’s identity. Housing stock ranges from more approachable executive homes to higher-end custom properties.

Buyer Considerations

Buyers evaluating Anthem typically weigh view orientation, lot size, proximity to the club, and HOA structure. Micro-location within the broader Anthem area can matter significantly for both lifestyle and resale.

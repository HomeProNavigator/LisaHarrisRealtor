Welcome to North Las Vegas

North Las Vegas is Nevada’s fourth-largest city and one of the faster-growing communities in the country. In the northern Las Vegas Valley, it mixes established neighborhoods with newer master-planned communities and generally more approachable price points than many parts of Henderson or Summerlin.

Originally incorporated in 1946 with industrial roots, the city has invested in infrastructure, parks, and residential development. Proximity to Nellis Air Force Base, the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, major freeways, and the rest of the valley makes it practical for commuting while offering a more relaxed pace than the urban core.

Lifestyle & community

The community is diverse and family-oriented, with a strong presence of first-time buyers and households seeking value. Craig Ranch Regional Park, recreation centers, and events at the park amphitheater support outdoor and community life. Newer villages often add their own pools, trails, and neighborhood programming.

Pros include relative affordability, newer master-planned inventory, freeway access, and growing retail. Trade-offs can include variable school quality by zone, higher crime in some older pockets, fewer dining and entertainment options than Summerlin or the Strip corridor, and aircraft noise near Nellis in certain areas.

Housing & real estate

Pricing typically runs below the Las Vegas metro average, which is a primary draw for first-time buyers and value-focused move-up households. Product ranges from older stock to brand-new construction.

  • Aliante — Master-planned community with parks, trails, golf, and Aliante Casino; townhomes through larger single-family homes.
  • Tule Springs — Newer northwest growth near the fossil beds monument; modern plans and energy-efficient construction.
  • Craig Ranch — More established area around the regional park with a mix of housing types and generally more affordable entry points.

New construction continues in northern and western sections. Appreciation has been meaningful in newer communities as buyers hunt for value across the valley.

Schools

North Las Vegas is served by Clark County School District; quality varies by neighborhood. Charter options such as Coral Academy of Science and Somerset Academy are frequently researched by families. Public names that come up include Eva Wolfe Elementary, Aliante Master Academy, and Legacy High School. Always verify current ratings and zoning for a specific address.

Amenities, dining & recreation

Craig Ranch Regional Park (trails, sports fields, dog park, amphitheater), Aliante Nature Discovery Park, and multiple recreation centers serve daily life. Golf options include Aliante Golf Club and nearby courses. The Las Vegas Motor Speedway and casino entertainment at Aliante and the Cannery add regional attractions. Shopping clusters around Aliante, North Decatur Marketplace, and the nearby Las Vegas Premium Outlets North.

At a glance

  • Population: 275,000+
  • Median household income: roughly mid-$50Ks
  • Cost of living generally below the Las Vegas average
  • Commute to the Strip: about 20 minutes depending on exact location and traffic

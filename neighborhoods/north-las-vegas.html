Welcome to North Las Vegas

North Las Vegas has emerged as one of the valley’s primary growth corridors for buyers seeking newer homes and more approachable price points. Master-planned communities, industrial and logistics employment, and improved freeway connectivity have reshaped the city’s residential story over the past two decades.

Areas such as Aliante, Tule Springs, and Craig Ranch offer distinct lifestyle profiles — from golf-oriented master plans to newer northwest subdivisions near open desert and the fossil beds monument.

Lifestyle & community

Residents often prioritize space, newer construction, and value relative to Summerlin or central Henderson. Parks, recreation centers, and community amenities are strong in the master-planned pockets. Proximity to I-15 and the 215 Beltway supports commuting across the valley and to employment centers near Nellis in certain areas.

Housing & real estate

The housing runs from older resale to new construction in Aliante, Tule Springs, and Craig Ranch. Do not use a valley-wide median as the price of a house here. A comparative market analysis is the number that matters. Call (702) 523-3336.

  • Aliante — Master-planned community with parks, trails, golf, and Aliante Casino; townhomes through larger single-family homes.
  • Tule Springs — Newer northwest growth near the fossil beds monument; modern plans and energy-efficient construction.
  • Craig Ranch — More established area around the regional park with a mix of housing types and generally more affordable entry points.

New construction continues in northern and western sections. Appreciation has been meaningful in newer communities as buyers hunt for value across the valley.

Schools

North Las Vegas is in the Clark County School District. The school for a house depends on the address, and zones change. Check the district for the current assignment before you treat a school name as a reason to buy. This page does not rank schools.

Amenities, dining & recreation

Craig Ranch Regional Park (trails, sports fields, dog park, amphitheater), Aliante Nature Discovery Park, and multiple recreation centers serve daily life. Golf options include Aliante Golf Club and nearby courses. The Las Vegas Motor Speedway and casino entertainment at Aliante and the Cannery add regional attractions. Shopping clusters around Aliante, North Decatur Marketplace, and the nearby Las Vegas Premium Outlets North.

At a glance

  • Owner-occupied homes: 63.6% in the 2020–2024 Census period.
  • Median value of owner-occupied homes: $404,400 for 2020–2024. That is not the list price of a new Aliante or Tule Springs home.
  • Southern Nevada, not this city alone: Las Vegas REALTORS’ August 2026 median sale for an existing single-family home was $475,000. A North Las Vegas house can sell under or over that. Call (702) 523-3336.

The Approach

How we prepare your home for the market

1. Market Intelligence

We begin with a detailed comparative analysis of recent sales, active competition, and absorption rates specific to your micro-location — not broad city averages.

2. Pricing Strategy

Pricing is set to attract the right buyer pool quickly while protecting value. Overpricing costs more than most sellers realize; underpricing leaves money on the table.

3. Presentation

Professional photography, targeted staging recommendations, and a marketing plan calibrated to the property’s price tier and audience.

4. Negotiation & Closing

Offers are evaluated against your goals. Contingencies, appraisal risk, and timeline are managed with the same rigor as the listing itself.
